# Conversion, virality and retention benchmarks for freemium gifting/social web products (for WISH BOARD revenue model and experiments)

Labels used on every finding: **[Measured]** = a count or rate the company or a data firm measured; **[Company claim]** = the company's own framing, no method shown; **[Estimate]** = third-party guess or aggregator; **[Experiment]** = A/B or lab result; **[Opinion]** = practitioner rule of thumb. The year of the data is given in brackets. Research date: 2026-11-22.

## 1. Free-to-paid conversion for freemium consumer products, one-off gifting/celebration purchases, and creator/fan tipping

### Takeaway
No gifting or celebration company found publishes its free-to-paid rate. The usable anchors are SaaS freemium benchmarks: about 3–5% counts as good, and those figures are mostly B2B. Against them sits one hard consumer data point. Deco My Tree, a Korean viral Christmas-message product, reached about 10M users but earned only about USD 20k on the App Store, so a cosmetic upsell inside a free viral moment converted far below 1%. Kudoboard instead charges per board at creation (USD 6.99/9.99/19.99), and that model shows what a one-off price ladder can look like.

### Cited Findings
**General freemium benchmarks (mostly SaaS, used as an upper bound)**
- [Measured, 2026] ChartMogul's conversion report covers about 200 software products. It reports a median free-to-paid rate around 8% and notes that few products sit near the median. — [ChartMogul Conversion Report](https://chartmogul.com/reports/saas-conversion-report/)
- [Opinion, widely cited] For freemium self-serve, about 3–5% is "good" and higher is "great". This rule of thumb is attributed to Ada Chen Rekhi's benchmark post; the search summary mixed it with ChartMogul, and I could not confirm which source says it. — [Ada Chen Rekhi, Freemium and Free Trial Conversion Benchmarks](https://medium.com/ada-chen-rekhi/freemium-and-free-trial-conversion-benchmarks-9fab38d096da)
- [Estimate, 2021–2025] First Page Sage averaged 80+ SaaS clients at 3.7% freemium-to-paid, ranging from 2.6% in EdTech to 5.8% in RegTech. This is reported second-hand. — [Crazy Egg, Free-to-Paid Conversion Rates Explained](https://www.crazyegg.com/blog/free-to-paid-conversion-rate/)
- [Opinion] A 2% rate may be "phenomenal" for complex enterprise software but "concerning for a simple consumer app". Most consumer products are freemium, while B2B skews to free trials. — [Crazy Egg](https://www.crazyegg.com/blog/free-to-paid-conversion-rate/)

The page shows no permanently free board; the "Start for Free" button sits on the USD 6.99 Lite tier. — [Kudoboard Pricing](https://www.kudoboard.com/pricing)
- [Estimate, unverified] Third-party aggregators put Kudoboard's revenue at roughly USD 2–2.3M a year, list about USD 14M in funding, and describe "individual boards and subscription packages" with a freemium approach. The sources disagree and none is primary. — [PitchBook profile](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/442324-18); [techlist.ai](https://techlist.ai/kudoboard.com)

- [Estimate, Japan 2024] A JR East Planning survey forecasts Japan's 応援広告 (fan "support ads") market at about JPY 76.9B for 2024, about twice 2023 and about 17% of Japan's out-of-home and transit ad spend. — [AdverTimes, 2025-11-01](https://advertimes.com/20251101/article520606)

### Inferences
- Deco My Tree's numbers suggest a very low paying share. USD 9.5k of in-app purchases over about 10M users is about USD 0.001 per user. If the average order were about USD 3 (assumed from "popular items under USD 5"), that is about 3,000 orders, or about 0.03% of users. The article may count only iOS App Store revenue, so the true figure could be several times higher, but it would still be well under 1%. **A free viral celebration tool with optional cosmetics should be modelled at 0.03–0.3% of users paying, not at SaaS-style 3–5%.**
- Kudoboard shows the other extreme: the board itself is the paid unit at creation, and the first real price is the lowest tier. WISH BOARD's planned KRW 3,000–10,000 upgrade sits almost exactly on Kudoboard's USD 6.99/9.99 ladder after currency conversion, which supports the price range as plausible for a one-off celebration purchase.
- Suggested modelling bands for paid conversion **per board creator** (not per visitor): low 0.3% (cosmetic-only, Deco-like), base 1–2%, high 3–5% (a clear paid "upgrade" at the moment of sending, with gift-quality features such as video or a prime screen). The high band leans on SaaS freemium benchmarks, so treat it as optimistic for consumers.
- Real Korean fans already pay KRW 1–7M a month for subway screens, and there are thousands of idol ads a year. A virtual "prime screen" plus a shareable video is a cheap substitute for that habit, so fan-team purchases may convert better than personal birthday boards. That is a hypothesis to test, not a measured fact.
- Revenue formula for the model: Revenue = boards created × paid conversion × average order value + team or bundle sales. Example only: 50,000 boards × 1% × KRW 5,000 = KRW 2.5M. Seasonal peaks multiply boards created (see Q4).

### Gaps
- No published free-to-paid rate for Kudoboard, GroupGreeting, Punchbowl, Jacquie Lawson or any e-card company, and no verifiable revenue for GroupGreeting or Punchbowl.
- No user or revenue numbers for Korean 롤링페이퍼 services. Paid app-intelligence tools (모바일인덱스, 와이즈앱, Sensor Tower) would be needed.
- No reliable average Cameo order price for 2024–2026, and no reliable revenue figure.
- No primary data on the share of fans who pay on Korean idol-voting apps.
- No Korean market-size estimate for fan billboard ads.

## 2. Viral coefficients and share/open rates for send-to-recipient products

### Takeaway
Measured K-factors are rare. The published ones are mostly blog anecdotes, and practitioners treat 0.15–0.25 as good and 0.4+ as great for consumer internet products. The strongest relevant evidence is Korean: two "decorate my tree" link services, where each owner collected about 9–13 messages. In one of them, 75% of all users ended up creating their own tree, and both spread within days because messages were locked until Christmas. Spotify Wrapped shows the share intensity of a personal-moment product: about 2.5 shares per engaged user on day one.

### Cited Findings
**K-factor benchmarks**
- [Opinion/anecdote compilation, 2020] Reported K values: Slack 8.5, Facebook 7, Viddy about 1, Jelly Splash soft launch 0.92, Ladder 0.73, EchoSign 0.2, Extole referral programs about 0.05, Square 0.01. The compiler warns these come from "passing mentions in blogs" and were not rigorously measured. — [Saxifrage, K-factor benchmarks (2020-09-15)](https://www.saxifrage.xyz/post/k-factor-benchmarks)
- [Opinion] Rahul Vohra's consumer-internet guide: K of 0.15–0.25 is good, 0.4 great, about 0.7 outstanding. — [Saxifrage citing Vohra](https://www.saxifrage.xyz/post/k-factor-benchmarks)
- [Measured, Adjust app data, about 2020] Only about 30% of apps had a measurable K-factor, with a median of 0.45. Here K means organic installs per paid install (about 45 per 100), which is not invite-based virality. — [Saxifrage citing Adjust](https://www.saxifrage.xyz/post/k-factor-benchmarks)
- [Opinion] K = invites per user × invite conversion rate, and growth compounds only above 1. Cycle time matters: a lower K with a short cycle can beat a higher K with a slow one. — [Wikipedia, K-factor (marketing)](https://www.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-factor_(marketing)); [Amplitude, Going viral is overrated](https://amplitude.com/en-us/blog/going-viral-is-overrated.md)

**Korean "personal link, friends write, reveal on the day" services (the closest analogue to WISH BOARD's loop)**
- [Measured, 2021] '내 트리를 꾸며줘' launched 2021-12-19. Users sign up, get a tree with their name, share the link, and friends each decorate it and leave a message, which unlocks on Christmas Day. By 2021-12-23 at 02:00 there were 1.82M users and 21.9M messages; by 12:00 the same day, 1.86M users and 24.96M messages, so about 1.8M users in four days. It spread by social-media word of mouth and suffered server errors and a DDoS attempt. — [경향신문, 2021-12-23](https://khan.co.kr/article/202112231756011)
- [Measured, 2021–22] A later interview put tree creators at about 2.5M. This figure comes from the search summary; I did not fetch the page. — [아주경제, 2022-01-01](https://www.ajunews.com/view/20220101143023804)
- [Measured, 2023–24] Deco My Tree launched on the App Store 2023-12-20 and drew nearly 860k downloads on its peak day, 2023-12-25. After one year it had about 9.96M cumulative users, 7.52M tree creators and 65.13M messages; the average user received about 8 friend messages on Christmas Day. It reached the Top 10 social free chart in 8 countries and supports 14 languages. — [woshipm, 2024-12-22](https://www.woshipm.com/share/6160075.html)
- [Measured, 2023] The format was copied quickly. Deco My Tree faced a copying dispute with 산타파이브, who demanded it shut down. — [Daum/news, 2023-12-23](https://v.daum.net/v/20231223153107807)

**Personal-moment share loops**
- [Company claim/measured, 2025] Spotify Wrapped 2025 reached more than 200M "engaged users" (at least one story viewed) in about 24 hours, up 19% year on year. 2024 took about 62 hours to reach 200M. Day-one shares exceeded 500M (native shares, screenshots and downloads), up 41% year on year. The full 2024 campaign engaged 245M users. — [TechCrunch, 2025-12-04](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/04/spotify-says-wrapped-2025-is-its-biggest-yet-with-200m-users-in-its-first-day); [Music Business Worldwide](https://www.musicbusinessworldwide.com/spotify-wrapped-campaign-hit-200m-engaged-users-in-24-hours-a-19-yoy-increase)
- [Measured, 2021–22] Wordle grew from about 90 players on 2021-11-01 to more than 300,000 on 2022-01-03, then 2M–2.7M at the time of sale. The NYT bought it on 2022-01-31 for a "low seven figures" and later said it brought "an unprecedented tens of millions of new users". — [NPR, 2022-01-31](https://www.npr.org/2022/01/31/1077089945/nyt-wordle); [WEF](https://www.weforum.org/stories/all/wordle-game-new-york-times-business-plan/); [TechCrunch, 2022-05-04](https://techcrunch.com/2022/05/04/wordle-new-york-times-user-growth)
- [Measured, 1996–97, old data] Hotmail reached about 12M users in about 18 months, mainly through a footer on every outgoing email ("Get your free email at Hotmail"), and was sold to Microsoft for about USD 400M in December 1997. Reported marketing spend ranges from USD 50k to under USD 500k, and claims that K stayed above 1 are unverified. — [TechCrunch, 2009-10-18](https://techcrunch.com/2009/10/18/ps-i-love-you-get-your-free-email-at-hotmail)

**Korean viral web tests**
- [Company claim, date unclear] 케이테스트 (psychology/MBTI-style tests, started late 2020) says its tests average 5–10M participants each. The operator tracks MAU because channel attribution is hard, and monetises through advertiser inquiries and branded tests. — [Harper's Bazaar Korea](https://www.harpersbazaar.co.kr/article/77768)
- [Company claim, unverified, promotional] A post said to be by the 케이테스트 developer claims "KRW 2B revenue from one phone call". It promotes a paid course, so treat it as marketing. — [maily.so](https://maily.so/cobaetoo/posts/g0zm8gvkoql)

### Inferences
- **Contributions per board**: Deco My Tree averaged about 8.7 messages per tree (65.13M ÷ 7.52M), and '내 트리를 꾸며줘' about 13 per user within four days (24.96M ÷ 1.86M). For WISH BOARD, model about 8–13 visitors or contributors per celebration board in a peak season, and fewer off-season (an assumption).
- **Recipient/contributor → creator**: In Deco My Tree, 7.52M of 9.96M users (about 75%) created their own tree, so visiting a friend's link very often ended in making one. Taken with about 9 messages per tree, the in-season loop was well above K = 1. This is an inference: the article does not separate how many users first arrived as contributors.
- **Timed reveal is the common mechanic**: both tree services locked messages until 12/25. That creates a deadline and a return visit on the day, as Spotify Wrapped and Wordle do with timed or daily drops. WISH BOARD's birthday reveal video fits this pattern, and the "reveal on the day" lock is worth A/B testing.
- **Share design**: Wrapped counts screenshots and downloads as shares, and Wordle's spread is usually credited to its spoiler-free shareable result, though I did not find a source for the grid itself. A ready-made, vertical, image or short-video export of the celebration is probably the highest-leverage share asset.
- **Fast copying**: the format was cloned within two years, so first-mover advantage in a season is short. A brand and an owned social graph (fan teams) matter more than the mechanic.

### Gaps
- No measured open rate for e-cards or personalised links: the share of recipients who open, and the share who create their own (recipient→creator), for any e-card company.
- No primary data that Wordle's emoji grid drove growth.
- No measured K for 롤링페이퍼 or MBTI sites beyond the participation counts above.
- Spotify does not publish shares-per-viewer or new-user acquisition from Wrapped.

## 3. Retention benchmarks: D1/D7/D30 for social and community apps, daily check-ins, streaks, collective goals and timed events

### Takeaway
Typical US social and communication apps keep about 32% of users on D1, 12% on D7 and 6% on D30 (Q3 2025 medians). a16z's bar for a breakout social app is much higher: 60/40/25 is good, plus DAU/MAU of 40%+. Duolingo's data shows streak mechanics bring small but real lifts (a milestone animation added +1.7% on D7). One-off timed events can re-engage at scale: Streak Revival drew 15.4M learners in June 2026. No published data covers fan-voting apps' daily behaviour.

### Cited Findings
**Category medians**
- [Measured, Q3 2025 US cohorts, MWM catalog] Median D1/D7/D30 retention: Social & Communication 31.9% / 12.3% / 5.9%; Lifestyle & Well-being 23.6% / 9.6% / 4.8%; Media & Entertainment 24.9% / 8.0% / 3.4%; Games 36.6% / 9.3% / 2.9%. Social leads at every horizon except D1, where games are higher. — [MWM, Retention glossary (State of April 2026 report)](https://mwm.ai/glossary/retention)
- [Estimate, unverified] UXCam gives social medians of 40% / 18% / 12%, citing AppsFlyer and Adjust. A roundup citing Adjust and Sendbird puts social/messaging at D1 25–29%, D7 9–10%, D30 about 5%. — [UXCam](https://uxcam.com/blog/mobile-app-retention-benchmarks.md); [Lovable guide](https://lovable.dev/guides/what-is-a-good-retention-rate-for-an-app)

**Breakout consumer-social bar (VC benchmark)**
- [Opinion, 2023, a16z's Bryan Kim] For seed-stage consumer social, OK / Good / Great: D1 50/60/70%, D7 35/40/50%, D30 20/25/30%; W1 40/55/75%, W4 20/30/50%; DAU/MAU 25/40/50%+; L5+ (share of weekly actives on 5–7 days) 30/40/50%+; monthly user growth 20/35/50%. If more than 10–20% of early users come from paid acquisition, the strategy needs rethinking. The metrics become predictive only at a few thousand actives and need 30+ days of cohorts. — [a16z, How to Benchmark Your Social App (2023-03-03)](https://a16z.com/do-you-have-lightning-in-a-bottle-how-to-benchmark-your-social-app/)

**Streaks and daily habits (Duolingo)**
- [Experiment, 2022] A new streak animation with milestone-day versions raised the chance a brand-new learner was still active on D7 by +1.7%. Letting learners equip up to two Streak Freezes raised daily active learners by +0.38%. — [Duolingo Blog, How the Duolingo streak builds habit (2022-01-31)](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit/)
- [Measured, correlational, 2022] Learners who reach a 7-day streak are 3.6× more likely to complete their course, and more than 6M learners had streaks of 7+ days as of January 2022. — [Duolingo Blog](https://blog.duolingo.com/how-duolingo-streak-builds-habit/)
- [Measured, Q2 2026] Current User Retention Rate (share of current-user DAUs who return the next day) hit a record 84%, about +1 point year on year. — [CX Dive](https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/duolingos-user-retention-hits-all-time-high-as-app-boosts-stickiness/827499/)
- [Measured, timed event, June 2026] A one-time "Streak Revival" let lapsed learners restore their longest streak by completing three lessons. 15.4M learners revived a streak, including nearly 8M who had no active streak when it began. Management says these users retain better than a typical re-engaged cohort, with no figure given. — [CX Dive](https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/duolingos-user-retention-hits-all-time-high-as-app-boosts-stickiness/827499/); [Webull Q2 2026 call transcript](https://www.webull.com/news/15392596720296960)
- [Measured, 2025–26] Duolingo DAU passed 50M in Q3 2025 (+36% year on year), reached 52.7M in Q4 2025 (+30%) and 58.7M in Q2 2026 (+23%). Management attributes part of the slowdown to "increased focus on monetization". — [Duolingo Q3 2025 release](https://duolingo.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/duolingo-surpasses-50-million-daily-active-users-grows-dau-36); [CX Dive](https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/duolingos-user-retention-hits-all-time-high-as-app-boosts-stickiness/827499/)
- [Opinion] Streaks drive short-term bursts well but are "not always suitable for long-term engagement". A case study argues many users do one lesson only to save the streak. — [Yu-kai Chou](https://yukaichou.com/gamification-study/master-the-art-of-streak-design-for-short-term-engagement-and-long-term-success/); [case study](https://jiwonvyoo.github.io/portfolio/case/duolingo/duo.html)

**Seasonal moment products**
- [Measured, 2023–24] Deco My Tree's usage peaks for only about 10–15 days around Christmas and dips afterwards. Users return each year after a good first experience, and the app re-entered the charts before Christmas 2024. — [woshipm, 2024-12-22](https://www.woshipm.com/share/6160075.html)

**Fan voting / attendance**
- [Measured, app listings] Korean idol-voting apps tie attendance (출석) and daily free currency to voting, and also sell daily currency packs (최애돌 데일리팩, KRW 3,900/month). No DAU, retention or attendance data was found. — [아이돌픽 App Store](https://apps.apple.com/tw/app/%EC%95%84%EC%9D%B4%EB%8F%8C%ED%94%BD-idolpick/id1461429755); [최애돌 App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/%EC%B5%9C%EC%95%A0%EB%8F%8C-%EC%95%84%EC%9D%B4%EB%8F%8C-%EC%88%9C%EC%9C%84-%ED%88%AC%ED%91%9C-%EC%8A%A4%EC%BC%80%EC%A4%84-%EB%B0%B0%EA%B2%BD%ED%99%94%EB%A9%B4/id1131995698)

### Inferences
- For a web product with no app install, D1 will likely sit at or below the social median (about 30%), and D30 in low single digits, unless fan-team attendance creates a daily reason to return. For WISH BOARD's model: personal birthday senders are mostly one-shot users (retention close to seasonal or annual), while fan-team members are the retention base, to be judged on DAU/MAU and L5+.
- Duolingo's measured streak lifts are small per feature (+0.38% to +1.7%) but compound, and run on an enormous base. For a small product, streak features will not move revenue by themselves. The larger lever is a timed collective event, such as a Streak-Revival-style "team comeback week" or a birthday-month team goal.
- Seasonal celebration tools retain on an annual cycle (Deco My Tree). WISH BOARD should plan for "re-activation each season" (e.g., notify last year's creators before the same date) rather than daily retention for personal users.

### Gaps
- No public D1/D7/D30 for daily check-in (출석체크) features specifically, or for Korean fan-voting apps (최애돌, 아이돌픽, Idol Champ).
- No data on collective-goal mechanics (team progress bars) and retention, apart from Duolingo's leagues, which were not researched here.
- The "7-day streakers are 2.4× more likely to return the next day" and "Streak Wager +14% D7" claims appeared in search summaries, but I could not confirm them on the Duolingo page I fetched. Possible sources are [making.duolingo.com](https://making.duolingo.com/how-streaks-keep-duolingo-learners-committed-to-their-language-goals) and [Lenny's Newsletter](https://www.lennysnewsletter.com/p/behind-the-product-duolingo-streaks). Do not use them without checking.

## 4. Pricing psychology for micro-purchases, and seasonal demand peaks

### Takeaway
Evidence backs three tactics for a one-off upgrade at KRW 3,000–10,000:
- **A three-tier ladder with a decoy.** Adding a dominated option moved choices from 32% to 84% for the premium bundle in Ariely's Economist test.
- **Small-unit, "price of a coffee" framing.** It works for small amounts but backfires for large ones.
- **Multi-pack bundles.** Kudoboard prices a 5-board pack at about 30% off.

Demand peaks around shared dates in both the US and Korea: Christmas, Valentine's, and in Korea 빼빼로데이, 수능 and Lunar holidays. Birthdays and idol birthdays give a year-round base.

### Cited Findings
**Decoy / tiered pricing**
- [Experiment, about 2006–08, old data] Ariely's Economist test offered web-only for USD 59, print-only for USD 125 (the decoy) and print+web for USD 125. 16% chose web-only, 0% print-only and 84% print+web. With the decoy removed, the split became 68% web-only and 32% print+web. Secondary sources say about 100 MIT students took part. — [Wikipedia, Decoy effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decoy_effect); [The Conversation](https://theconversation.com/the-decoy-effect-how-you-are-influenced-to-choose-without-really-knowing-it-111259)
- [Measured, live pricing 2026-11-22] Kudoboard's ladder is USD 6.99 / 9.99 / 19.99, each tier "Everything in" the one below. The Milestone tier costs twice Premium for modest extras, which looks like a decoy (my reading). Struck-through prices and "save up to 60%" and "Save 36%+" badges frame bundles and annual plans. — [Kudoboard Pricing](https://www.kudoboard.com/pricing)

**"Price of a coffee" / pennies-a-day framing**
- [Experiment, 1998] Gourville's "Pennies-a-Day" (Journal of Consumer Research 24(4):395–408) shows that reframing an aggregate cost as small daily amounts changes how the transaction is judged and raises compliance, because it brings small everyday expenses to mind as the comparison. — [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/oup/jconrs/v24y1998i4p395-408.html)
- [Experiment, 2021] Participants were more likely to donate when the appeal said "$1 per day" rather than "$350 per year". — [Basu 2021, JESP (accepted manuscript)](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/176603/1/Basu_2021_AAM_JESP.pdf)
- [Experiment, 2018] Periodic pricing backfires for larger amounts: a USD 7/day expense can feel worse than its yearly equivalent. — [Atlas & Bartels 2018, JCR](https://home.uchicago.edu/~bartels/papers/Atlas-Bartels-2018-JCR.pdf)
- [Field experiment, 2018–19] Framing savings deposits as "$5 per day" rather than monthly or yearly amounts closed the participation gap between income groups. — [Hershfield, Shu & Benartzi](https://anderson-review.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/Hershfield_Shu_Benartzi_2018_SSRN-id3097468.pdf)
- [Company claim] Buy Me a Coffee's whole brand is the coffee-price anchor for one-off tips. Its blog argues small, frictionless one-time tips earn creators more. This is marketing, not independent data. — [Buy Me a Coffee blog](https://buymeacoffee.com/blog/earn-more-with-one-time-tips/)

**Bundles / multi-recipient packs and add-ons**
- [Measured, live pricing] Kudoboard's 5-board Premium Package costs USD 35/yr, USD 7.00 per board against USD 9.99 single. Gift-card attachment is an add-on from the lowest tier, and print (poster or book) and download are add-ons. — [Kudoboard Pricing](https://www.kudoboard.com/pricing)
- [Measured, 2023–24] In Deco My Tree, buyers mostly chose items under USD 5 even though items went up to USD 399.99, which suggests an impulse price band for virtual decorations. — [woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/share/6160075.html)

**Seasonal demand peaks**
- [Measured/estimate, US Greeting Card Association figures republished over years, mostly pre-2020] Americans buy about 6.5B cards a year (USD 7–8B). Christmas is the top seasonal occasion at about 1.6B cards, then Valentine's (145M, excluding classroom valentines), Mother's Day (133M) and Father's Day (90M). Birthdays are the top everyday occasion, about half of everyday cards; one outlier figure says 86%. — [Greeting Card Universe market research](https://www.greetingcarduniverse.com/content/greeting-card-industry-market-research); [USPS Link, 2019-09-26](https://news.usps.com/2019/09/26/facts-on-paper/)
- [Company claim, Korea, 2017] Kakao said 카카오톡 선물하기 passed KRW 1조 in annual transactions. Monthly transaction value was "noticeably higher" around Valentine's Day, White Day, 빼빼로데이, 수능, Christmas, 설날 and 추석. Kakao had sold 1.5M Pepero in one day (2015-11-11) and 1.7M the next year, which it called a single-product daily record. — [Kakao newsroom](https://www.kakaocorp.com/page/detail/8432)
- [Company claim, Korea] Kakao runs dedicated 수능 cheer campaigns ("수험생에게는 따뜻한 응원") and, in 2025, a 빼빼로데이 friend-invite sharing event with a top prize of 2M shopping points. — [Kakao newsroom (수능/빼빼로)](https://www.kakaocorp.com/page/detail/11805)
- [Measured, Korea] Idol birthday ads run all year: about 12,433 idol subway ads in one year in Seoul. — [데일리안](https://www.dailian.co.kr/news/view/1694327/)

### Inferences
- Suggested ladder for testing: KRW 3,900 / 6,900 / 12,900, with the top tier as decoy or anchor (e.g., top tier = prime screen + long video + downloadable keepsake). Add a "friends' birthday 5-pack" at about 30% off, as Kudoboard does, and a "커피 한 잔 값" label only on the lowest tier, since the framing backfires on larger amounts.
- Expect personal-board volume to spike in Korea around 2월 졸업, 2/14, 3/14, 5/15 스승의날, 11/11 빼빼로데이, 수능 day (mid-November) and 12/25, with birthdays spread across the year. The fan-team side runs on idol birthdays. Twelve thousand idol subway ads a year is about 34 a day in Seoul alone (simple division), which suggests a steady flow of fan "occasions".
- An exchange-rate check on Kudoboard: USD 6.99 is about KRW 9–10k, so WISH BOARD's KRW 3,000–10,000 band sits at or below the global one-off board price.

### Gaps
- No published experiment on gift add-ons (e.g., attaching a gift card) and their effect on conversion.
- No Korean-specific pricing experiment for micro-purchases on the web; the closest are app-store price points.
- No 2022–2026 US greeting-card occasion data; the GCA figures are old and inconsistent between republications.
- Kakao has published no breakdown of transaction value by season since 2017.

## 5. Case studies of small web products that monetised a viral personal moment

### Takeaway
Viral moments spike for days to weeks, and money comes either from selling scarce placement during the spike (Million Dollar Homepage: about USD 1.04M in five months) or from selling the asset (Wordle: low seven figures within three months). Free "personal link" tools with cosmetics or ads earn very little even at 10M users (Deco My Tree: about USD 20k). The '내 트리를 꾸며줘' team needed donations to cover about KRW 7M of server costs. Cameo shows the downside of a pandemic-boosted moment: its valuation fell about 90%+ within three years.

### Cited Findings
- [Measured, 2005–06, old data] The Million Dollar Homepage (Alex Tew, UK student, launched August 2005) sold pixel ad blocks and grossed USD 1,037,100 in about five months. The last 1,000 pixels went on eBay on 2006-01-01 and sold 2006-01-11 for USD 38,100. Tew expected net income of USD 650–700k after costs, tax and a charity donation. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Million_Dollar_Homepage); [The Register, 2006-01-12](https://www.theregister.com/2006/01/12/million_pixels)
- [Measured, 2021] '내 트리를 꾸며줘' was a side project by five people. It reached about 1.8M users and about 25M messages in four days in December 2021, cost about KRW 7M in extra servers, and was funded by donations with ads planned. — [경향신문](https://khan.co.kr/article/202112231756011)
- [Measured, 2023–24] Deco My Tree reached about 10M cumulative users and 65M messages in a year and about USD 20k of App Store revenue (peak day USD 6,780 on 12/25/2023). Each season's peak lasts 10–15 days, and users return yearly. The developer, Unboxers, has shipped 9 "small but fun" products in about two years. — [woshipm](https://www.woshipm.com/share/6160075.html)
- [Measured, 2021–22] Wordle went from about 90 players to millions in about two months. It was free with no ads, and its creator sold it to the NYT for a low seven-figure sum about three months after the surge began. The NYT says it brought "tens of millions" of new users. — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2022/01/31/1077089945/nyt-wordle); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/05/04/wordle-new-york-times-user-growth)
- [Measured, 2021–2024] Cameo, a pandemic-era personalised video greetings boom, was valued at over USD 1B in 2021 and under USD 100M in March 2024, with headcount going from about 400 to under 50. It is now pivoting to creators and a TikTok integration. — [Fortune](https://www.fortune.com/2024/07/30/cameo-app-unicorn-silicon-valley-startup-celebrity-influencer-social-media/); [TheWrap](https://thewrap.com/cameo-layoffs-50-employees-remain); [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/2026/03/31/cameo-tiktok-creator-partnership/)
- [Company claim, unverified] 케이테스트 (Korean viral tests, launched late 2020) monetises through branded tests and ads. A self-promotional post claims about KRW 2B in revenue. — [Harper's Bazaar Korea](https://www.harpersbazaar.co.kr/article/77768); [maily.so](https://maily.so/cobaetoo/posts/g0zm8gvkoql)
- [Measured, 2022–26] Duolingo's DAU growth slowed from +36% to +23% as it leaned into monetisation, a sign of the growth-versus-monetisation tension even for a scaled habit product. — [Duolingo Q3 2025 release](https://duolingo.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/duolingo-surpasses-50-million-daily-active-users-grows-dau-36); [CX Dive](https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/duolingos-user-retention-hits-all-time-high-as-app-boosts-stickiness/827499/)

### Inferences
- **What monetised**: scarce, visible placement sold during the spike (Million Dollar Homepage pixels, which resemble WISH BOARD's prime screens), branded or sponsored content (케이테스트), or selling the product. **What did not**: optional cosmetics in a free message tool (Deco My Tree, about USD 0.002 per user).
- WISH BOARD's billboard slots map onto the Million Dollar Homepage model (scarce, public, permanent-feeling placement) better than onto the Deco My Tree model. Making the paid tier about *visibility and scarcity* (prime screen, time slot, keepsake video) rather than decorations is the better-supported bet. This is inference from two cases, not a controlled comparison.
- Spikes last 10–15 days (seasonal) to a few months (Wordle, Million Dollar Homepage). The revenue model should be a sum of seasonal spikes plus a fan-team base, not a smooth monthly curve.
- Infrastructure cost lands during the spike (KRW 7M for '내 트리를 꾸며줘' in four days). WISH BOARD's budget guard (Meter DO lean/hard) is consistent with this risk; the payment flow should be in place before the season, not added during it.

### Gaps
- No revenue reports for Korean 롤링페이퍼 graduation-season services, personalised song or video sites, or 산타파이브 after 2021.
- No data on how much of the Million Dollar Homepage's revenue came in the first weeks versus later (only the total and final auction).
- No primary Cameo revenue series to size the decline.
